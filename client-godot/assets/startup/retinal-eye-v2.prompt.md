# Eye texture generation record

Method: built-in `image_gen` tool, using the user's vintage green monochrome eye screenshot as the anatomy/composition reference and previous startup screenshots as negative references. Continuous-tone grayscale textures are converted to teal/black halftone by `StartupEye.gdshader` at runtime.

Open texture: `retinal-eye-v2.png`. Closed texture: `retinal-eye-v2-closed.png`.

## Open-eye prompt

Create a production grayscale texture for an animated retro eye-scanning game screen. Input references: the USER-UPLOADED vintage green monochrome software screenshot is the composition and anatomy reference; the other images are current Godot startup-screen screenshots and are NEGATIVE references for the eye shape (too symmetric, too small an iris, too artificial eyebrow and uniform shading). Output ONLY a single natural photographic close-up of one human eye and surrounding skin, landscape 3:2, edge to edge, no UI, no red box, no text, no border. Continuous-tone grayscale, NOT pixel art or dithering; the engine supplies one-bit halftone later. Reproduce the vintage reference eye's organic asymmetrical shape and framing closely. One eye viewed nearly straight on with a slight oblique tilt; eye's outer corners are at approx (0.10,0.62) and (0.91,0.49) of image. Upper lid slopes/arches organically, highest at (0.54,0.29); bottom lid at approx (0.52,0.76). Center of iris and pupil at (0.52,0.52). Large natural medium-light iris diameter about 32 percent image width / 48 percent image height, partially tucked under upper lid. Pupil is a small dark round circle about 34 percent of iris diameter, not enlarged or dilated. Rich but soft natural radial iris structure. No large white glints in pupil; a tiny soft reflection on upper iris is fine. Eye opening spans roughly 80% image width and 39% image height. Surrounding skin and socket occupy over half the image, with organic photographic tonal gradients: bright upper-left forehead/skin, deep shadow beneath upper lid especially upper-right, dark outer corner, illuminated lower lid with soft under-eye creases, pronounced darker lower-right socket shading. Natural sparse short lashes that merge into upper lid shadow, not a mascara outline. No detached thick dark eyebrow bar: crop eyebrow mostly out of frame, only subtle brow/socket shading near top. Skin photographic texture and realistic subtle creases; broad light/dark areas must survive conversion into a coarse 1980s monochrome halftone. Match reference's eye proportions, pupil-to-iris ratio, asymmetry, and surrounding-skin ratio rather than an idealized vector almond. Avoid illustration, perfectly symmetric almond, concentric geometric iris rings, full floating iris circle, giant pupil, stylized eyebrow arch, green colors, duplicated eyes, camera hardware, UI, labels, film grain overlays. Save as a reusable sharp texture.

## Closed-eye edit prompt

Edit this exact grayscale eye photograph to create its CLOSED-EYELID animation frame. Preserve exact camera, 3:2 aspect ratio, crop, skin position and texture, eyebrows, surrounding shadows, lighting, contrast and every area outside the eyelids. Change ONLY the eyelids/eye opening: close this same eye naturally in a relaxed blink with the upper lid coming down, lashes meeting the lower lash line, no iris or pupil visible. Anatomically natural closed eyelid with subtle skin folds; do not squeeze or scrunch the eye, do not add new makeup or eyelashes. The closed lash seam should follow the original lower eyelid's gentle tilted curve, with the same eye corner positions. Photo-real continuous-tone grayscale. This is a matching animation texture, not a new photo or recrop. No text, UI, pixelation, dithering, borders or red box.
